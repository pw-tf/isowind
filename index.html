What it is

The whole of Morrowind, seen from the sky

You keep your own legal copy of the game. Isowind runs on top of it: a top-down camera, optional pixel-art rendering, controller-friendly menus, and a full multiplayer stack based on TES3MP.

Top-down camera W is always up the screen. Aim weapons and spells at the mouse cursor while your character runs the way you move. Mouse wheel zooms; F7 cycles views.
Two looks Pixel art draws the world at low resolution with chunky pixels and retro textures from your Morrowind files. Or keep Morrowind’s usual graphics.
Mouse or controller Menus adapt to whichever you use. Controllers get their own layouts, an on-screen keyboard, and quick keys on the bumpers.
Multiplayer A port of TES3MP. Host co-op (one shared world) or MMO (private loot, quests, companions; enemies respawn; parties).

Ready to play from above?

Download the package, point it at your Morrowind install, and start. Windows and Linux (including Steam Deck) are supported.
